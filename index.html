Who we are

A response built by the people it serves.

WORD works across emergency and recovery settings in South Sudan — integrating health, education, protection, nutrition, livelihoods, WASH, shelter and peace building.

Founded by professionals and survivors of conflict, we collaborate with authorities, community leaders, NGO partners and donors for accountable, community-led delivery.
