Who we are

A response built by the people it serves.

Women's Organisation for Resilience and Development (WORD) works across complex emergency and recovery settings in South Sudan, integrating humanitarian intervention across food security and livelihoods, shelter and non-food items, education, WASH, protection and gender-based violence response, health, nutrition, and peace building.

WORD was founded by professionals and survivors of conflict, and operates in close collaboration with national, state and county-level authorities, community and religious leaders, NGO partners, and donors.
