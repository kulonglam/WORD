How we work

Three principles that shape every program.

WORD designs interventions that multiply through households, centre survivors, and shift harmful norms with local leaders.

01

Ripple effect

Empowering one woman impacts an entire household. A mother given trade skills becomes economically independent, and better able to support her children's nutrition, health care, and school fees.

02

Survivor-centred

Programs are designed with the direct input of the women WORD serves, so that interventions stay culturally sensitive and safe.

03

Local advocacy

WORD works alongside community leaders and traditional authorities to challenge harmful norms, including child marriage and denial of property rights.

Organisation values

What WORD holds itself to.

  • Empowerment

    Giving women the tools to solve their own challenges.

  • Integrity

    Maintaining transparency and accountability to donors and communities.

  • Inclusivity

    Serving the most vulnerable, regardless of ethnic or religious background.

  • Courage

    Standing firm in advocacy for women's rights, even in fragile environments.

  • Sustainability

    Focusing on long-term solutions for lasting change.
