Donate or fund a programme.

Your support helps WORD deliver food security, shelter, education, WASH, protection, health, nutrition and peace-building work with women and girls.

How to give

WORD is a newly registered national NGO. Online card donations are not yet enabled. To contribute or discuss a funding partnership:

  1. Email word94091@gmail.com with the subject “Donation / funding enquiry”.
  2. Or use the contact form and select Funding / donation.
  3. We will share banking details, acknowledgement process and any due-diligence documents you need.

What your support funds

  • Life-saving and recovery programmes across eight pillars.
  • Women- and girl-centred protection and livelihood support.
  • Accountable operations in partnership with local authorities.
