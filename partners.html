Who we work with

Four circles of partnership.

Government

Communities

Community leaders, religious leaders, women and girls who shape and guide our interventions.

NGO partners

National and international organisations collaborating on multi-sectoral humanitarian and recovery work.

Donors

Funders and philanthropic partners who enable accountable, community-led programmes.
