How we measure

Reach, quality and lasting change.

Reach

Women, girls and households supported across our pillars of intervention.

Quality

Survivor-centred, culturally safe delivery with community feedback.

Change

Improved livelihood, protection, health, education and social cohesion outcomes.

Intended outcomes

What success looks like across each pillar.

  • Health

    Timely access to essential and curative care for women, girls and households.

  • Education

    Higher girl-child enrolment, adult literacy pathways, and dignity support that keeps girls in school.

  • Protection & GBV

    Safe case management, psycho-social support and legal pathways for survivors.

  • Nutrition

    Malnourished children reached through basic feeding and recovery support.

  • Food security

    Skills, VSLA participation and climate-smart livelihoods that strengthen household resilience.

  • WASH

    Safer water access and menstrual hygiene support for women and girls.

  • Shelter & NFIs

    Verified distribution of shelter materials and essential non-food items.

  • Peace building

    Local conflict resolution that reduces tension and builds social cohesion.
