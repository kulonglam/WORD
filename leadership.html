About

Leadership and governance.

WORD is women-led and accountable to the communities we serve, our partners, and South Sudan's regulatory framework.

Founding

WORD was founded by professionals and survivors of conflict and registered by the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission on 1 April 2026 (Reg. No. 6338).

Governance principles

  • Women's leadership at the centre of decision-making.
  • Accountability and transparency to donors and communities.
  • Survivor-centred programme design.
  • Collaboration with national, state and county authorities.
